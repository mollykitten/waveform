What you get in 0.3

⌘K palette

Search tracks, people and playlists as you type. Paste a soundcloud.com link and it opens in the app.

0-6s

Gapless playback, optional crossfade

Waveform loads the next track in the background and starts it on the last sample of the current one.

Media keys

Control Center on macOS, SMTC on Windows, MPRIS on Linux. Your headphones' pause button works.

Your password stays with SoundCloud

You sign in on SoundCloud's own page. The app keeps only a session token, on your disk.

A queue you can edit

Drag to reorder, remove what you skip. Shuffle keeps a history, so previous goes back to what you heard.

Listening stats

Top artists, genres and hours from your last 400 plays, counted on your machine. Turn on Last.fm to scrobble.

Install

macOS

waveform-macos.dmg

Signed and notarized, so Gatekeeper opens it without asking. Needs macOS 12 Monterey or later.

Windows

waveform-windows.zip

Unzip and run Waveform.exe. SmartScreen asks once: click More info, then Run anyway.

Linux

waveform-linux.tar.gz

Extract with tar xzf and run ./waveform_app.

Test builds go to the Telegram channel first: t.me/waveformsc
